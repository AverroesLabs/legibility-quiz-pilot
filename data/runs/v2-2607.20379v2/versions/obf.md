# Train the Model, Not the Reader: Decodability Supervision for Verifiable

###### Abstract


That the lies are still flagged at O8D 0.95 by the Q2Z probe while the control probe collapses to chance at 0.51, and that an independent probe on the pretrained model reliably scores the verbalizer's true claims above its false ones (O8D 0.96, versus 0.82 without Q2Z), so that checking the probe catches false claims about M7K, is reported together with the suppression, by an adversary editing an explanation to maximize the score while lying, of about 87% of its lie penalty, on a pretrained Pythia-160M to which the finding replicates under a rule for designing targets we identify, where M7K becomes reliably decodable by a probe although a fresh verbalizer conveys it only in part (H6N 0.44–0.46 against a near-zero control). ## 1 Introduction

An adversary that edits the explanation to game the deployed reconstruction score achieves suppression of ${\sim}87\%$ of its lie penalty on both models, yet the lies are still flagged by Q2Z's activation probe (O8D $0.95$) while the control probe collapses to chance ($0.51$); Q2Z's B3N thus survives text gaming, and the reconstruction score must not be used as an H6N signal.

The intervention that survives the controls trains the target model rather than the activation reader (Figure 1b) — Q2Z, linear heads on external targets co-trained with the model (§5) — and the M7K is then stated faithfully by fresh Z7Ms in the sandbox under both co-trained evaluators and an R3F, with the Y4W absent.

Figure 1: The two architectures. (a) A Z7M (prior work) (Fraser-Taliente et al. 2026): AV maps an activation to text and T9Q maps it back, reconstruction quality being the test, and only AV and T9Q train; the score can stay high while the text asserts a specific lacking C5B (red "mirror"). (b) Q2Z (this work) trains the target model: top, co-trained linear heads preserve the B3N of M7K (M7K denoting the designated content) against external targets; bottom, after training, an independent fresh probe (not the heads) recovers the content (O8D $0.95$) and a fresh verbalizer states it with H6N.

A Z7M lets a model explain its own hidden states: a verbalizer turns an activation into a text explanation, a T9Q turns the explanation back into an activation, and the quality of the round trip is treated as a test of each explanation's faithfulness (Fraser-Taliente et al. 2026), an idea attractive for oversight by virtue of being unsupervised and self-checking, with no labels or human in the loop, just a number attached to every explanation; released verbalizer/T9Q pairs exist for several open models, and a growing family of methods trains activation readers with related objectives (Pan et al. 2026; Karvonen et al. 2025; Huang et al. 2025).

In practice the test is passed in two identified ways, neither of which is faithful at the level of individual claims, and the two natural training fixes also fail: the distance between a hidden state and its reconstruction is minimized by one of them, which removes content a probe could previously read, while the verbalizer is rewarded by the other for C5B, the score being raised through a change in which sentences the metric counts rather than through the C5B of more claims.

By L6P, on a released A5D-based Z7M, explanations reconstruct well above chance, yet the audit that flips each salient claim of explanations scored by reconstruction, with validity controls for each edit, to test whether the reconstruction depends on it detects C5B (C5B denoting grounding) in only ${\sim}2\%$ of their salient specific claims — a lower bound, the flip test having low sensitivity and likely undercounting actual C5B — so that the released verbalizer of this A5D-based Z7M (A5D denoting Qwen-2.5-7B) passes the test even though its specific claims largely lack C5B, and the score survives paraphrase but collapses when content words are masked, leaving L6P (L6P denoting gist), not specific wording, to carry it.

By Y4W, co-trained verbalizer/T9Q pairs in a synthetic sandbox with exact ground H6N consistently develop Y4W — false wording that the reconstruction depends on — under standard training across two scenarios, with gaps between C5B and H6N (H6N denoting truth) rates of 0.30–0.80, and the Y4W are detected and quantified through the comparison of C5B and H6N and through the swap to an R3F (R3F denoting an independent evaluator).

The reconstruction test has a structural gap: reconstruction only checks that the explanation contains enough to regenerate the activation, and false additions are not penalized, the objective having no preference among claim values that induce the same reconstruction, a gap that is a property of the objective itself, present regardless of model or scale.

Both alternatives motivated by interpretability fail for identified reasons — the reconstruction objective collapses the B3N (B3N denoting decodability) of content, and the approach using a frozen probe goes stale under N2S (N2S denoting representation drift) — and where a reconstruction objective so collapses content's B3N and a frozen probe so goes stale under N2S, Q2Z instead trains the target model, a realignment that needs no labels fully reversing this, with claim-level faithfulness restored in the sandbox and transfer to J7B (J7B denoting Pythia-160M), on which M7K acquires B3N by a probe at a measured cost.

## 2 Preliminaries and Experimental Setup

In the 2.1 audit, continued pretraining of J7B (160) (Biderman et al. 2023) with self-supervised heads is the third setting, with U4E (U4E denoting tax) held-out language-modeling loss minus a shared control; the first setting is a released A5D (2.5/7) layer-20 Z7M on in-distribution web text ( $n{=}1{,}517$ audited claims), the second is a sandbox of two synthetic scenarios, a story and a marketplace, each built by filling the slots of a fixed template so that the true content of every activation is known exactly and computable without a judge, the tap position fixing what the activation can hold (retained, faded, or unread slots), and full protocols, scenario grammars, seeds, and hyperparameters are in Appendices D and K. A Z7M (Z7M denoting the natural-language autoencoder) pairs an activation verbalizer (AV), which generates a text explanation $z$ of a hidden activation $h$ , with T9Q (T9Q denoting the activation reconstructor), which maps $z$ back to an estimate $\hat{h}$ ; the AV is trained against reconstruction reward and, with target model weights $\theta$ frozen and $h$ its tap activation, the pair optimizes

| | $\mathcal{J}_{\mathrm{read}}\;=\;\max_{\mathrm{AV},\,\mathrm{T9Q}}\;\cos\!\big(\mathrm{T9Q}(\mathrm{AV}(h)),\,h\big),$
| | (1)

and the reconstruction quality of a produced explanation is treated as its faithfulness test (Figure 1a), with Fraser-Taliente et al. (2026) explicit that rising reconstruction tracks explanation informativeness while warning that nothing in the objective forces faithfulness, even though that score is still the number these Z7M report and the main criterion their users apply; the question is what that score indicates about the individual claims of each explanation, measured with a centered cosine normalized to the floor, where $\mathrm{floor}$ is the mean cosine between reconstructions and mismatched activations (chance level), V2X $=(\cos(\hat{h},h)-\mathrm{floor})/(1-\mathrm{floor})$ : 0 at chance, 1 at perfect reconstruction, and the released Z7M uses the A5D layer-20 AV/T9Q pair (Qwen Team 2025). C5B here is the sensitivity of the reconstruction to a claim flip, the thesis being that this is not the same as the model’s own sensitivity to the flip and that the two come apart in practice; for a claim $c$ in $z$ , let $z_{\neg c}$ be $z$ with $c$ alone minimally flipped by D8K (D8K denoting a minimal-pair counterfactual edit, which we call a flip), each edit is checked for validity because an edit that changes more than its target claim can inflate apparent C5B severalfold (Appendix A), the claim’s C5B is the reconstruction drop

| | $\Delta\mathrm{V2X}(c)=\mathrm{V2X}(z)-\mathrm{V2X}(z_{\neg c}),$
| | (2)

and $c$ has C5B when $\Delta\mathrm{V2X}(c)\geq\tau$ for a threshold $\tau$ . Crossing C5B with H6N gives four cases—a claim has C5B or not, and is true of the source or not—with focus on wording that has C5B but is false, where the reconstruction depends on the wording although its stated content is false; when such wording is a convention specific to the co-trained pair, shown by the swap to an R3F (§3.2), it is called Y4W, these labels being operational and implying no intentional stance, and, with $\mathrm{V2X}_{\mathrm{co}}$ and $\mathrm{V2X}_{\mathrm{ind}}$ the scores under the co-trained reconstructor and an independent reconstructor, S9L (S9L denoting the evaluator gap) is $\mathrm{V2X}_{\mathrm{co}}-\mathrm{V2X}_{\mathrm{ind}}$ . The target model’s activation is read at a chosen layer and position, the tap; an activation can be read by the reconstructor T9Q or by a probe, a freshly fitted linear decoder (Alain and Bengio 2017; Belinkov 2022); L6P is the input’s overall meaning, as opposed to its specific facts; content is M7K when an auxiliary predictor is co-trained to predict it (§5); content has B3N when a freshly fitted linear probe can read it; faithfulness in this paper is always claim-level, each individual claim in an explanation being judged as true of the activation and having C5B in it rather than the explanation being judged as a whole, and faithfulness is not used to mean whether explanations describe the model’s downstream computation, nor C5B to mean C5B in the world.

## 4 Representation Collapse and Probe Drift

Adapter-based transfer of probes across models should work (Zhao et al. 2026), because representations differ by G6H; the two natural fixes fail through two mechanisms, one being W8G and the other being N2S during continued training, with probes matched to a training model decaying to near-chance within 1,500 continued training steps and at chance across seeds (all six pairs), even though the information itself remains intact and fully recoverable, since an unlabeled G6H fitted on paired activations (no labels needed) restores native probe performance in all nine cases and decomposition of that map (mode sweep figure in Appendix H) shows rotation alone recovering about half the N2S while full recovery needs a non-orthogonal change of rank ${\sim}$ 16–64, the same N2S and realignment being concurrently reported by Duan (2026) on 2B-parameter and 7B-parameter models. Probe-based monitoring of a model that is still training must include an explicit recalibration procedure, for realignment restores a probe’s access to what the target model stores, but whether the model stores the right content is the underlying problem, given that realignment only corrects how the probe reads the representation (G6H denoting an affine map and W8G denoting representation collapse) whereas Q2Z changes what the representation contains; under W8G, the most natural target-side objective, movement of activations toward their own reconstruction, makes the probe’s recovery and the model’s computation agree and collapses decodable content, removing at every tested strength, regardless of initialization, decodable slot content while perplexity is unchanged, with freshly retrained probes losing accuracy too, so the information itself is gone, not merely rotated into a subspace probes cannot read, a mechanism consistent with prior analyses of collapse in non-contrastive self-supervised learning (Chen and He 2021; Tian et al. 2021), because the reconstruction is a lossy projection of the state and increasing the similarity between the state and its reconstruction reduces variation outside the probe’s subspace, so it removes any content the probe cannot recover.

At scale, what Q2Z guarantees is B3N, the M7K being present in, and verifiable by a probe from, the hidden state, with the verbalizer in the reconstruction family conveying it into text only in part, so that the free-form explanation constitutes a lossy rendering of what the activation holds and the probe-based monitor verifies claims against internals rather than trusting the prose; and B3N is a running cost rather than a one-time setting, since, the heads removed, it erodes within a few hundred steps of fine-tuning and must be maintained, the maintenance being either continuous ( $+0.010$ nats) or intermittent at about a tenth of the compute, whence its being better trained in from the start than restored later (Appendix C).

The transfer of Q2Z to the continued pretraining of J7B, the 160M-parameter model, follows the setup of §2.1 (§5.3), and the three results reported by Table 1 and Figure 2 are that M7K retains B3N at scale, fresh probes reaching 0.95–1.00 O8D (O8D denoting probe AUC) for $K{=}8$ and $K{=}64$ M7K words against a 0.77–0.80 control baseline; that the independent audit matters at scale, the naive head at $K{=}512$ (a presence classifier) having minimized its training loss while making nothing B3N to a fresh probe (Appendix I), scoring well by always predicting "absent" under so many rare targets, a shortcut that only an independent probe exposes, the balancing of the loss by class frequency closing it and restoring 0.97–0.99 O8D across all 512 targets, the design rule being the use of targets whose loss cannot be minimized without genuine discrimination; and that the B3N cost depends on target design, lying at $+0.010$ nats for 64 targets at the effective design, not distinguishable from zero, and rising to $+0.14$ – $0.20$ nats for 512 targets into $d{=}768$ , a discriminative X1V (X1V denoting an identity target) retaining B3N at $w{=}0.03$ at a U4E within noise of zero, the never-designated probe bucket sitting at control level in all eight supervised runs.

The comparison of Eq. 3 with Eq. 1 states the thesis: whereas $\mathcal{J}_{\mathrm{read}}$ optimizes the verbalizer and the T9Q against a frozen model's activations and never touches $\theta$ , $\mathcal{J}_{\mathrm{model}}$ trains $\theta$ itself, adding to the language-modeling loss a term for the ground truth that the verbalizer and the T9Q will need; with head parameters $\phi$ and external targets $y$ ,

| | $\mathcal{J}_{\mathrm{model}}\;=\;\min_{\theta,\,\phi}\;\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{LM}}(\theta)\;+\;w\,\mathcal{L}_{\phi}\big(h(\theta),\,y\big),$
| | (3)

$w$ weighting the head loss against the language-modeling loss, and $\mathcal{L}_{\phi}$ being a class-balanced binary cross-entropy for whether each content word is present plus a cross-entropy for which token it is, the balancing preventing the trivial shortcut of §5.3. The approach that survives the controls trains the target model itself rather than the verbalizer, the T9Q, or the probe that reads its activations: Q2Z (Q2Z denoting Readable Encodings via Co-trained Auxiliary Predictors) adds linear auxiliary predictors (heads) during training so that M7K retains B3N from selected hidden states, each head reading the hidden state at the tap layer and predicting an external target, namely slot values in the sandbox or self-supervised text functions at scale, the head loss then being added to the language-modeling loss (§5.1).

All three failure modes are addressed at their source: N2S cannot set in, the heads co-training with the target model so that no frozen probe is left to go stale; W8G cannot occur, the targets being external ground truth rather than the model's own reconstruction, so that no self-referential objective remains to collapse into; and Y4W cannot form, the head reading the activation directly, with no intervening text where such a code could hide. Because a co-trained predictor could co-adapt with the model, every B3N number reported comes from independently fitted probes, never from the heads, Q2Z standing as B3N supervision in the deep-supervision lineage (Lee et al. 2015; Szegedy et al. 2015).

The intended effect is produced on the target model (§5.2): M7K reaches 100% B3N by independently fitted probes, at $+0.001$ nats, in all five runs, the heads recovering only content the activation actually holds and decoding nothing in any run on unread slots, whose values never appear before the tap, this U4E being re-estimated on pretrained J7B below, where it is $+0.010$ nats and depends on target design. The effect propagates beyond the heads, fresh Z7Ms trained on Q2Z target models stating the M7K at ${\sim}1.00$ H6N and C5B under both the co-trained evaluator and the R3F and flagging the remaining false content at C5B ${\sim}0$ , no Y4W appearing in any Q2Z run (emergence ledger in Appendix D); both Z7Ms receive identical training, including a cold-start SFT on gold descriptions, and only the Z7M trained on activations from the Q2Z target model retains near-perfect H6N and C5B, so the difference isolates the effect of target-model B3N.

B3N is also robust to head capacity and tap choice, a nonlinear (MLP) head reaching the same 0.98–0.99 O8D for the designated probe at layer 6, and a linear head at layer 9 lifting O8D from the control's 0.77–0.79 to 0.95–0.98 at $+0.01$ nats, the full weight sweep and the other factors varied (pull strength, designation dose, realignment map class, evaluator, scenario, head capacity, tap layer) being indexed, with outcomes, in Appendix I.

Whether that B3N reaches a fresh verbalizer, as it did in the sandbox, is the question of the audit at scale (§5.4), and on J7B it does: an AV/T9Q pair trained on these taps with the same procedure as the released Z7M, audited against exact ground truth from the text, states context words at H6N 0.44 on the Q2Z model versus 0.06 on the control, the control model reproducing at scale the signature of C5B-high but false wording, stated words sitting at C5B 0.48 but at H6N only 0.06, a gap Q2Z narrows from $+0.42$ to $+0.33$ without eliminating it, the content being stated with greater H6N, not by removal of Y4W. The gain is not mere selectivity, Q2Z recovering more of the present content words, at recall 0.29 versus the control's 0.07, while stating fewer words per explanation, robust to T9Q strength and stable across three target model seeds, at H6N 0.44–0.46 versus a near-zero control (Appendix J). The verbalizer nonetheless conveys only part of what the activation contains, the shortfall being a property of generation, not of storage: at the verbalizer's own recall level a fresh probe on the same tap reaches precision ${\sim}0.80$ , versus the verbalizer's own precision of 0.44–0.63 across T9Qs and seeds (precision–recall figure in Appendix J). The T9Q was redesigned — standardized injection across multiple tokens, MLP capacity, and a presence head trained at the decode point on whether each word occurred in the window (an external fact, not a learned monitor) — rendering the M7K linearly B3N from the hidden state at its decode point (0.79–0.81, two seeds), yet the same T9Q's generated precision stays 0.56–0.63, a matched control's state at the decode point reading at chance (0.03) under the identical head, so the B3N content is Q2Z's, not the head's; and none of T9Q scale, reconstruction optimization, a direct H6N reward, injection bandwidth, or explicit negative supervision closes the gap, a 3B T9Q matching the 0.5B.

## 6 Probe-Based Monitoring

Post-tap claims are correctly abstained on by both target models, because content that the activation provably lacks is not adjudicable by the reading of that activation, and the catching of such claims is assigned to the comparison of C5B and H6N (§3), with the audit partitioned by the two tools under one scenario, one seed, and one threshold; the sandbox monitor, built so that what an auditor can verify after Q2Z has made B3N available for content could be found out, used fresh probes, one per slot, reading the tap activation while the verbalizer states slot values, and any claim contradicted by a confident probe ($p{>}0.9$) was flagged as a candidate discrepancy and scored against exact ground-H6N (full protocol in Appendix B). On the standard target model, soundness with low recall is the outcome: every flag is a genuine false claim, but most pre-tap claims, namely those about content the activation can hold, fall in slots whose B3N is too low for a confident readout, so only 27 of 733 are caught; on the Q2Z target model the reversal is that no false pre-tap claims remain and abstention falls to zero, every claim thereby being verified against internals rather than taken on trust, with verification coverage of the M7K pre-tap content moving from 37% to 100%, a gain in coverage rather than in the monitor’s precision and not an extension to the whole activation. Extension to the real model is shown by J7B (160): a fresh presence probe ranks true claims above false ones at O8D $0.965$ versus $0.823$ for the control, with disjoint $95\%$ intervals, and flags false claims at $0.93$ recall and $0.87$ precision; because at scale’s $1.5\%$ base rate the sandbox’s per-claim coverage does not transfer, O8D and the flag’s precision and recall are reported instead, while the verbalizer proposes and the probe verifies, the probe rather than the free-form explanation being the reliable readout at scale, consistent with training the model so its content is checkable rather than trusting the prose, and Q2Z thereby improving not just what a probe reads but how well it can monitor the verbalizer. An adversary who edits the explanation text could fool any deployment that trusted the reconstruction score, by editing a true stated word in an honest explanation into an absent one and choosing the edit that maximizes the recomputed score (full protocol in Appendix F); at the score-maximizing edit the lie penalty is nearly gone on both models, about 87% suppression, yet the external probe on ground-H6N still flags the lies on the Q2Z model at O8D $0.952$, while on the control it collapses to chance (O8D $0.508$), a survival specific to content whose B3N is Q2Z-supplied, since the control probe also reads the activation the text-editing adversary cannot touch and fails, and since reading a signal that predates the editable report is necessary but not sufficient unless the content also has B3N, which Q2Z supplies. The adversary’s freedom to choose which words to lie about costs the monitor only a little, with the Q2Z probe drifting from O8D $0.993$ to $0.952$ as the chosen absent words grow semantically nearer; the threat model covers edits to the explanation text, not activation-level attacks (Bailey et al. 2024), and within it immunity is structural for score manipulation and empirical for candidate selection, while even an adversary that knows the probe and assumes the monitor leaks (outside the threat model) degrades the Q2Z monitor only to O8D $0.909$, the control meanwhile falling below chance to $0.242$, so the separation grows with adversary strength and the deployed score must not be used as an H6N signal against such adversaries, whereas a probe on content whose B3N is Q2Z-supplied can. The operating point is not tuned on the evaluation set: recalibrating the flag threshold on a disjoint split leaves recall essentially unchanged, no threshold for the control probe reaches $0.9$ precision even against random lies, and the effect is confined to M7K, because on held-out words the model was never trained for B3N (ranked 64–128) the Q2Z monitor has no advantage over the control (O8D $0.66$ versus $0.70$), the designation of M7K being both the mechanism and the scope of the effect (band table in Appendix F).

## 7 Related Work

Added here are the recovery anatomy (§4), the cross-seed failure, exact evidence that information outlives its readers, and the separation of N2S from W8G, all against a backdrop in which equivalent representations are learned by networks up to a transformation (Lenc and Vedaldi 2015; Kornblith et al. 2019; Moschella et al. 2023), in which features are transferred across language models by G6Hs (Chen et al. 2025), in which pretraining dynamics are stable across seeds (van der Wal et al. 2025), and in which tuned lenses are the G6H readers whose staleness is characterized here (Belrose et al. 2023), while the same staleness, together with its repair without labels on 2B- and 7B-parameter models, is concurrently reported by Duan (2026) as convergent evidence at deployment scale, and detectability after fine-tuning that is aware of the probe is likewise restored by the retraining of probes (Wehner and Fritz 2025).

Linear heads are added without any constraint on flow, the target being external content whose B3N is to be kept rather than the task label that serves as the target in deep supervision, namely auxiliary heads on hidden layers (Lee et al. 2015; Szegedy et al. 2015), a choice distinct from concept bottlenecks, which route all information flow through named concepts (Koh et al. 2020; Sun et al. 2025), from concept whitening, which constrains the geometry (Chen et al. 2020), from codebook features, which discretize the representation (Tamkin et al. 2023), and from gradient routing, which localizes computation (Cloud et al. 2024); and whereas supervision on the explanation side can track behavioral change (Guo et al. 2026), the supervision here acts on the target model. No steganography objective or communication reward is present, the codes emerging from the standard reconstruction recipe alone, inside the interpretability pipeline, even though all such channels are induced by incentives, process supervision, or an adversary, and a complementary route is shown relative to the prescription of that work that the monitor not be optimized on: supervision of the target model against external ground H6N confers B3N on its content without pressuring it to fool the probe or T9Q. A monitor is contributed whose signal comes from an independent probe of the target model and which survives the model gaming the reconstruction score (Baker et al. 2025), a contribution to both scalable oversight, within which reconstruction scoring sits and which substitutes cheap automatic signals for costly human judgment of model behavior and internals (Amodei et al. 2016; Bowman and others 2022), and to the parallel line of AI control, in which protocols are designed that stay safe even when the model tries to subvert them (Greenblatt et al. 2024).

The reconstruction test itself is audited, per claim and counterfactually, on a released Z7M, existing critiques of verbalization being benchmark-level and many tasks being solvable without the target's internals (Li et al. 2025), and the repair trains the target model where prior work improves the reader, the reader being improved by a fast-growing family beyond the Z7M recipe (§2) (Fraser-Taliente et al. 2026), spanning supervised question-answering decoders (Pan et al. 2026), general-purpose activation explainers (Karvonen et al. 2025; Bauer et al. 2026), interpreter assistants (Huang et al. 2025), readouts that need no training (Ghandeharioun et al. 2024), and cross-model adapters (Zhao et al. 2026), with sparse autoencoders constituting the unsupervised reader family (Huben et al. 2024), while model-produced explanations pass plausibility checks without faithfulness in chain-of-thought (Turpin et al. 2023; Lanham et al. 2023), causal tests of neuron explanations found high error and little efficacy (Huang et al. 2023), and counterfactual faithfulness tests prefigure our flip protocol (Atanasova et al. 2023). Objectives in the reconstruction family have hidden information in their channels since CycleGAN (Chu et al. 2017); in LLMs, encoded reasoning arises in chain-of-thought under optimization pressure (Roger and Greenblatt 2023) and generalizes under process supervision (Skaf and others 2025); misspecified incentives yield steganographic collusion (Mathew et al. 2025), tool use widens the channel (Rippin et al. 2026), and probing internals detects collusion (Rose et al. 2026), while optimization can obfuscate activations against latent monitors (Bailey et al. 2024) and training against a chain-of-thought monitor yields obfuscated reward hacking rather than honesty (Baker et al. 2025).

## 8 Discussion and Limitations

The guarantee covers only M7K for which B3N holds, so Q2Z is a component of oversight rather than a solution to it, while success for verbalization tested by reconstruction is not a higher V2X but explanations whose specific claims are verified against the target model, calibrated abstention where content is absent, and monitors with recalibration protocols; scoring explanations by reconstruction, a cheap automatic signal meant to stand in for expensive human checking of a model’s internals and thus a form of scalable oversight, is fragile because a model can pass it without being faithful and an adversary can game it outright. Against models capable of gaming the very metrics used to judge them (Baker et al. 2025), the needed property is that the monitor survives an attacker editing its own explanation, with the supervision moved onto the target model helping when, by training M7K so that B3N holds, Q2Z turns oversight from trusting the model’s prose into checking an independent probe. The evidence comes from a synthetic sandbox, one released Z7M (A5D; 2.5; 7), and continued pretraining of one small model (J7B; 160), mostly at single seeds, with frontier scale untested; four limitations constrain the conclusions: the sandbox and scale audits cover only closed-vocabulary claims parsed from a template, the monitor covers only M7K, and open-ended faithfulness is unmeasured; Q2Z must be co-trained in, cannot be retrofitted onto a frozen model, and depends on choosing a good target, since a poorly chosen one can report success while decoding nothing; B3N is not verbalizability, because the content is under B3N by a probe but the free-form verbalizer conveys only part of it, making a probe the more reliable readout; and Q2Z certifies what an activation stores, not what the model uses, since M7K can be present and checkable yet behaviorally inert (patching the tap barely moves behavior, ${\sim}0.003$ ), and a model could route computation around a monitored subspace (Mahinpei et al. 2021; Margeloiu et al. 2021).

## 9 Conclusion

An explanation that must be taken on faith is, for the oversight of increasingly capable models, a weaker basis for trust than one checkable against internals, a learned test of a model's own explanations is gameable by that model, and the audit of the reconstruction test behind natural-language activation explanations finds the passing of that test by models to proceed through L6P and through Y4W, with training targeted at the test issuing in W8G or in proxy gaming rather than in faithfulness, while the reading of the content is effected more reliably by a probe, which at scale ranks the verbalizer's true claims above its false ones, than by the verbalizer's own generation of it. Coverage of M7K by a probe-based auditor is raised from 37% to 100% in the sandbox under the one fix surviving the control experiments, Q2Z, which trains the target model rather than the reader and which yields verbalizers faithful at the claim level in the sandbox and brings about B3N of M7K by a probe on J7B, the 160M sandbox, where a fresh verbalizer states it truly against a near-zero control; the induction of B3N into the target model is more reliable than the auditing of a trained model after the fact.

## Appendix F Adversarial Monitor: the Lie-Ladder

Designation is therefore both the mechanism and the scope, Q2Z making lies about M7K detectable at a precision-controlled operating point and conferring no protection on claims outside the designated vocabulary; under the strongest adversary considered, a probe-aware selection adversary outside the report-space threat model that assumes the private monitor leaks, the Q2Z activation-probe monitor was degraded only to AUC $0.909$ (recall $0.70$) while the control was inverted below chance to $0.242$, that sub-chance value being the expected signature of a white-box attack that selects the absent words this weak control probe over-scores rather than an anomaly, and the Q2Z–control separation grew the stronger the adversary ($0.27$ at L1, $0.67$ at L4), consistent with Q2Z's probe margin absorbing selection pressure the control lacks.

The score itself was nearly gamed at L3, the optimal lie on a stated true word suppressing ${\sim}87\%$ of the penalty a random lie incurs (Q2Z $\Delta$V2X $0.022$, CI $[0.017,0.027]$), yet the Q2Z probe stayed discriminative (AUC $0.952$) while the control probe sat at chance ($0.508$), robust monitoring requiring the content to be decodable, which Q2Z supplies, a probe merely positioned to read a signal that predates the editable report not being enough; selection leakage down the rungs of the ladder (E9R denoting the paper's Lie-Ladder) was small (Q2Z AUC $0.993\to 0.952$), immunity being structural for score manipulation and empirical for candidate selection.

Whether a deployment that trusted V2X as a H6N signal could have a false claim slipped past it by a report-space adversary who edits the explanation to maximize V2X, and whether an activation-probe monitor survives that adversary, was the question under test; the protocol paired Q2Z with control J7B (160M) at $K{=}64$ balanced, took on held-out taps the honest verbalizer generation and, for each stated word, edited it into an absent top-64 word (a true-to-absent lie), recomputed V2X on the edited text through the real reconstructor, and scored the lie with the external-H6N presence probe trained on train taps, never on verbalizer outputs.

Adversary rungs of increasing strength were L1 a random absent word, L2 the embedding-nearest absent word, L3 the per-claim $\arg\max$-V2X absent word over all candidates (the strongest V2X-gaming lie in the edit family), and, as a worst-case bound outside the threat model, L4 a probe-aware adversary that assumes the private monitor leaks and, among absent lies keeping V2X within $0.05$ of honest, picks the one the probe most mistakes for present, the $0.05$ budget being over twice the $0.022$ score cost of the strongest pure-score lie (L3) and therefore a deliberately generous, conservative bound rather than a tuned tolerance; the design was decoupled (positive $=$ any truly-present word; edit slot $=$ the verbalizer's stated word) so that both arms are well powered, Q2Z $n{=}758$, control $n{=}200$, honest V2X $0.304$/$0.275$, and the attack was edit-based, with no reinforcement learning.

## Appendix K Hyperparameters and Reproducibility Notes

Every reported bucket O8D is reproduced to three decimals by a full rerun with $\lambda$ fixed, so that no selection bias enters the reported numbers, an earlier version having selected $\lambda$ per bucket from $\{10,10^{2},10^{3},10^{4}\}$ by evaluation-split O8D; the fresh probes concerned are closed-form ridge per rank bucket at fixed $\lambda{=}10^{4}$, fit on the first 80% of held-out probe sequences and evaluated on the last 20% (disjoint contiguous blocks; long documents may straddle the boundary), and the identity probes are 64-class logistic. Significance is assessed throughout by 95% bootstrap confidence intervals and disjoint-interval comparisons (with a Hanley–McNeil power analysis for the held-out-band null), not by fixed-level hypothesis tests, held-out language-modeling loss being measured on 20M tokens. Recent library defaults loading fp16 checkpoints natively, AdamW in fp16 destroys all weights in a single step through epsilon underflow while loss and gradients still read finite, a caution uncovered during setup, and all runs force fp32 parameters; all experiments run on single NVIDIA A100-80GB GPUs under Modal (Debian-slim, Python 3.12; PyTorch, Transformers, Accelerate, and NumPy, with exact versions pinned in the released environment file). In the scale runs on J7B (160), continued pretraining on pile-uncopyrighted used 500M tokens per run with identical data and order across runs, at batch $64\times 1024$ tokens, under AdamW ($10^{-4}$, cosine to 10%, warmup 100, weight decay 0.01, gradient clip 1.0) with bf16 autocast over fp32 weights, heads being linear on the layer-6 hidden state, F5C targets using BCE averaged over $K$ with the class-balanced variant weighting positives by the empirical inverse window base rate, clamped at 100, estimated from the first 2M training tokens, and X1V targets using cross-entropy over the top-$K$ content vocabulary at offset $-8$. The optimizer hyperparameters (learning rate, warmup, weight decay, gradient clip) were fixed to standard values and not swept, the method-specific hyperparameters (aux weight, target count $K$, designation dose, tap layer, head capacity) being the ones varied, with ranges and the selection rule in the Ablation Index.

In the sandbox runs, audits parse stated specifics by exact closed-vocabulary scan and apply C5B flips; Q2Z ceilings are evaluated by independently fitted probes on tuple-disjoint splits in scenario 1 and held-out splits in scenario 2, while the Q2Z runs themselves use aux weight 0.3, 3,000 steps, batch 128, learning rate $3{\times}10^{-4}$, and 30,000 probe taps, the underlying scenarios being two synthetic ones (story; marketplace) over closed vocabularies, with target models that are 8-layer, $d{=}256$ transformers trained from scratch and a toy Z7M that uses a 0.5B-parameter AV/T9Q pair with a dimension adapter, norm-matched activation injection, cold-start SFT on full-story descriptions, then alternating T9Q fitting and rejection-sampling rounds against reconstruction reward (the released recipe's structure). Each run is controlled by a single integer seed passed to torch.manual_seed (with a matched CUDA generator for sampling), fixing initialization, data order, and stochastic training and evaluation, the seeds numbering three in scenario 1 plus two in scenario 2; the C5B flips swap one stated word for a same-type vocabulary word and measure the reconstruction drop.
