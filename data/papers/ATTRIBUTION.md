# Papers used in this study

Every paper here is an arXiv preprint released under a Creative Commons licence (CC BY, CC BY-SA or CC0), which is why the selection rule (`docs/paper-selection.md`) kept only those. Copyright stays with the authors.

**Changes we made.** `text.md` is the paper converted from arXiv HTML to plain text with images removed (`scripts/paper_text.py`). The rewritten versions in `data/runs/*/versions/` (obfuscated, compressed, compressed + obfuscated, key insight implicit, key results removed) and the `-rem` folders here were written by language models for this study. They deliberately distort the papers and must not be cited as the authors' work. Versions of CC BY-SA papers are shared under CC BY-SA 4.0.
